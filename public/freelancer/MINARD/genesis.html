I. Primeval History (Gen 1–11)

16 places across Mesopotamia, Anatolia, Arabia & the Levant: Garden of Eden, Havilah, Cush, Asshur, Land of Nod, Mountains of Ararat, Land of Shinar, Babel, Erech (Uruk), Accad, Calneh, Nineveh, Calah, Sidon, Lasha, and Sephar.

II. Abraham & Sarah (Gen 12–25)

26 places tracing the Covenant journey: Ur of the Chaldeans, Haran, City of Nahor, Shechem, Bethel, Ai, Negev, Sodom, Gomorrah, Zoar, Valley of Siddim, Hebron (Mamre & Machpelah), Dan, Damascus, Salem & Mount Moriah, Beer-lahai-roi, Gerar, and Beersheba.

III. Isaac, Jacob & Esau (Gen 25–36)

9 distinctive sites (plus shared patriarchal centers): Wells of Esek, Sitnah & Rehoboth, Galeed / Mizpah in Gilead, Mahanaim, Peniel (Ford of the Jabbok), Succoth, Ephrath / Bethlehem (Rachel's Tomb), Migdal-Eder, and Bozrah in Edom.

IV. Joseph & Egypt (Gen 37–50)

7 distinctive sites (plus Hebron, Shechem & Beersheba): Dothan, Gilead spice route, Adullam & Chezib, Timnah & Enaim, On (Heliopolis), Land of Goshen (Rameses), and the Threshing Floor of Atad (Abel-Mizraim).
