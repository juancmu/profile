About Minard’s 1869 Masterpiece

Published by retired French civil engineer Charles Joseph Minard (1781–1870) on November 20, 1869, the Carte Figurative is celebrated by Edward Tufte as potentially the best statistical graphic ever drawn. It weaves six variables into a single two-dimensional surface:

  • Troop Headcount: Represented by the width of the flow zones (one millimeter per 10,000 men on the original print).
  • Geographic Coordinates: Latitude and longitude across Lithuania, Latvia, Belarus, and Russia.
  • Direction of Movement: Warm chamois/gold for the advance toward Moscow; dark charcoal for the catastrophic retreat.
  • Army Detachments: Macdonald’s X Corps to Riga in the north, Oudinot/Saint-Cyr’s II & VI Corps at Polotsk, and the main Grande Armée column.
  • Dates & Key Battles: From crossing the Niemen on June 24, 1812, to re-crossing at Kaunas on December 14, 1812.
  • Freezing Winter Temperatures: Plotted on the Réaumur scale below the map, showing how the −20°R to −30°R (−25°C to −37.5°C) freezes devastated the retreating survivors at the Berezina and Molodechno.

Adaptations Made to the Dataset

This interactive map directly loads the three workspace GeoJSON datasets in data/ and enriches them with historical context:

  • data/napoleon-campain.geojson (45 MultiPolygons): Rendered with proportional stroke/fill styling and interactive troop attrition calculations. Corrected the route metadata label on the 145,000-troop Smolensk–Dorogobuzh advance segment.
  • data/napoleon-Adv.geojson (13 Waypoints): Standardized bilingual dates (e.g., Ago → Aug), reconciled Old Style/New Style calendar ordering around Borodino (26 Aug OS / 7 Sep NS) and Mojaisk, and linked local troop estimates to each waypoint.
  • data/napoleon-ret.geojson (14 Waypoints): Reordered chronologically from the surprise attack at Tarutino (18 Oct) and evacuation of Moscow (19 Oct) to the final bridge crossing at Kaunas (14 Dec). Standardized place spellings (Balrus → Belarus, Kaunas (Latvia) → Kaunas (Lithuania)), translated and expanded the Vilnius retreat entry into full English while preserving the original Spanish note, and paired retreat stops with Minard's temperature readings.
